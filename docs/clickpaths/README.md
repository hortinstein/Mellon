## <a id="02-provision-lock"></a>Provision a lock

Create a lock anonymously from the console, using the built-in classification example.

**1.** Empty "Provision lock" form. Click "Load classification example".

![Provision a lock step 1](02-provision-lock/01.png)

**2.** The NIGHTJAR example fills the form. Optionally open "Decision thresholds".

![Provision a lock step 2](02-provision-lock/02.png)

**3.** Thresholds can be tuned per lock (defaults shown as placeholders). Click "Create lock".

![Provision a lock step 3](02-provision-lock/03.png)

**4.** Created: the owner token is shown once (and saved in this browser); the lock appears in the registry.

![Provision a lock step 4](02-provision-lock/04.png)

**5.** The Adjudication panel opens for the new lock, with its marking and rules of engagement.

![Provision a lock step 5](02-provision-lock/05.png)

![Owner tools step 2](04-owner-tools/02.png)

**5.** …the request is refused with an in-page error.

![Owner tools step 6](04-owner-tools/06.png)

## <a id="05-accounts"></a>Accounts and Mellon sign-in

**1.** Identity panel, signed out. Click "Create account".

![Accounts and Mellon sign-in step 1](05-accounts/01.png)

**2.** Choose a username and write identity rules: how you will prove who you are.

![Accounts and Mellon sign-in step 2](05-accounts/02.png)

**3.** Signed in: the nav shows @alex; locks provisioned now are linked to the account.

![Accounts and Mellon sign-in step 3](05-accounts/03.png)

**4.** Provision a lock while signed in…

![Accounts and Mellon sign-in step 4](05-accounts/04.png)

**5.** …it appears under "Your locks", and in the registry as @alex.

![Accounts and Mellon sign-in step 5](05-accounts/05.png)
